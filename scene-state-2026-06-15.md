## The platform decision (live, not yet locked)

The streaming-platforms decision is the most actively-evolving piece.
There are two docs in Coda:

- `docs/infra/streaming-platforms.md` — older version, includes StreamLicensing.com
  in the shortlist (which shut down in 2019; this is now wrong)
- `docs/infra/streaming-platforms-2026.md` — newer version, corrected to:
  Live365 ($59–$999/mo, PRO bundled) | Radio.co ($15–$49/mo, narrower PRO
  coverage) | DIY + AzuraCast (~$54–129/mo equivalent, full control) | DIY +
  bare Liquidsoap/Icecast (leaner)

**The current recommendation in the newer doc:** DIY + AzuraCast is the
right shape for 2026 launch. Cost-comparable to Live365, full control, no
listener-hour overage surprise. PRO agreements via ASCAP/BMI/SoundExchange
inquiry letters (templates in `pro-inquiry-templates.md`).

**The mini already runs AzuraCast** (verified today via the gateway status
output). So this decision is effectively made for the architecture — the
remaining question is whether the public stream lives on AzuraCast directly
(needs PRO compliance handled by Gray Shows) or relays to Live365/Radio.co
(managed PRO).
